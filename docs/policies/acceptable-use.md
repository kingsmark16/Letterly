# Letterly acceptable use (draft)

**Status: draft — legal and trust-and-safety review required before
publication.**

Letterly is for personal, lawful letters and interactive pages. Do not use the
service to threaten, stalk, harass, impersonate, exploit, or expose another
person; publish private or identifying information without permission; upload
malware; infringe intellectual-property rights; evade moderation; or use the
service for unlawful activity. Do not create content that sexualizes or
exploits children.

Visitors can report a public page without an account. Letterly may disable a
page or account when a report, investigation, or safety concern requires it.
Creators can contact support about a disabled page or account, but a support
request does not automatically restore access. Emergency or imminent-harm
reports should use local emergency services and the published safety contact.

The final published policy must define enforcement, appeals/support handling,
law-enforcement requests, and the applicable legal terms.
