# Letterly copyright and media policy (draft)

**Status: draft — legal review required before publication.**

Creators must own or have permission to use every letter, image, recording,
music track, and other media they upload or publish. A public link or a
purchase does not by itself grant permission to republish someone else's work.
Do not upload material that infringes copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity,
or other rights.

Rights holders may contact the published support address with the affected
URL, identification of the work, a good-faith statement, contact details, and
the required legal declarations. Letterly may restrict access while a claim is
reviewed and may retain the minimum moderation record needed to resolve it.

The final policy must include the designated notice-and-action process and
jurisdiction-specific requirements.
