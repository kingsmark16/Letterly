# Letterly child-safety policy (draft)

**Status: draft — child-safety and legal review required before publication.**

Letterly supports users of any age. The service must never be used to groom,
sexualize, exploit, threaten, or endanger a child, or to share sexual material
involving a child. Do not request a child's private contact details, location,
school, passwords, or images for an unsafe purpose.

Visitors and creators can report a public page without an account. Reports
involving suspected child exploitation or imminent danger are prioritized for
immediate trust-and-safety review and escalation to the appropriate authority
when required by law. Do not include unnecessary identifying details in a
report. Emergency situations should also be reported to local emergency
services.

The final policy must name the responsible safety contact, escalation windows,
preservation process, and jurisdiction-specific reporting duties. This draft
does not claim that Letterly can guarantee detection or prevent all harm.
